# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants in the Aqiron Security community are expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional. Discussions should focus on the work and its evidence, welcome good-faith questions, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and publication of private or sensitive information.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to project spaces and interactions where people participate in Aqiron Security. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior is abusive, threatening, discriminatory, or otherwise unsafe.

Maintainers should apply this standard consistently and proportionately, protect the privacy of people who report concerns, and document enforcement decisions where appropriate.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer at [opendeveloper.ai@gmail.com](mailto:opendeveloper.ai@gmail.com). Do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to explain the concern. If the concern involves that address, use another private channel provided by the project owner.
